## Description:
This project is a social music review platform designed for music listeners to rate, review, discover, and share their opinions about songs and albums. The application will allow users to create profiles, keep track of music they have listened to, and interact with other users. 

Users will be able to browse a music catalog, write reviews, give ratings, follow other users, and explore a social feed featuring music reviews and recommendations. The goal is to create a community where users can discover new music, exchange opinions, and connect with others who share their musical interests.

## User Stories:
As a music listener, I want to rate songs and albums, as well as write reviews, so that I can share my opinions and keep track of the music I enjoy.

As a music listener, I want to explore songs and albums reviewed by other users so that I can discover music I may enjoy.

As a music listener, I want to interact with other users who share my musical interests so that I can exchange opinions and discuss music.

As a music listener, I want to view a history of the songs and albums I have rated and reviewed so that I can revisit my opinions and see how my musical taste changes over time.

As a music listener, I want to follow users whose musical interests are similar to mine so that I can keep up with their reviews and recommendations.

As a user, I want to personalize my profile with a bio and profile picture so that other users can learn more about me and my musical interests and preferences.
